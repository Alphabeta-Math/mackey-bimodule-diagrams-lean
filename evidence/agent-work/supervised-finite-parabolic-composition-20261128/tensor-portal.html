Universal property of the tensor product for balanced maps into abelian groups

Statement

Let RR be a unital ring, MM a right RR-module, NN a left RR-module, and

τ:M×N⟶M⊗RN,τ(m,n)=m⊗n.τ:M×N⟶M⊗R​N,τ(m,n)=m⊗n.

The map ττ is balanced (Balanced maps from a right module and a left module, and bilinear maps over a commutative ring). For every abelian group AA and every balanced map b:M×N→Ab:M×N→A, there is a unique group homomorphism

b‾:M⊗RN⟶Ab:M⊗R​N⟶A

such that b‾(m⊗n)=b(m,n)b(m⊗n)=b(m,n) for all m,nm,n. Consequently composition with ττ is a bijection
